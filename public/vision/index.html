// privacy moat

Certify without ever exposing your data.

Synthetic test sets + private deployment open the regulated and classified markets cloud-only rivals can't touch — finance, healthcare, defense and government.

Synthetic test data

  • Generate realistic, fully fake test cases from your data's shape + a few scrubbed samples
  • Adversarial edge cases real data rarely contains — better stress tests
  • Or anonymized / perturbed real data when you do need it

Private deployment

  • Eval engine + AI judge run inside your walls — cloud, on-prem or air-gapped
  • The little real data you use never leaves your environment
  • Classified work: our tooling runs in your enclave; we ingest nothing

// defensibility

Why this compounds instead of commoditizing

Four moats that get stronger with every customer and every eval run.

Data moat

Your traces, rubrics and golden sets become your own definition of 'good.' Ripping us out means re-litigating what quality even means.

Loop moat

Owning certify → distil → route means we own the decision, not just the chart. Switching means re-proving every routing call.

Incentive moat

Savings-share aligns us with the buyer's CFO. Our revenue rises as we cut their bill — incumbents can't follow without cannibalizing seats.

Compliance moat

Synthetic data + private / on-prem deploy let regulated and classified buyers certify without exposing real data — a market cloud-only rivals can't serve.

// how it works

From eval to action in three steps.

1

Certify your model

Point us at your in-house model, fine-tune, RAG system or agent. We build the golden sets and grade it — synthetic data keeps your real records private.

tw certify ./model
2

Distil on your traces

We auto-train small student models on your own traces, then prove quality holds against the certified baseline.

tw distil --from traces
3

Route & cut the bill

Every step routes to the cheapest backend that passes. You watch frontier spend fall on a private dashboard — and own every line.

tw route --cheapest-pass
